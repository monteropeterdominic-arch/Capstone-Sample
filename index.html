How the study works

Two OLS models share eight explanatory variables: maximum temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, rainfall, distance to road, distance to river, distance to irrigation, and elevation.

  • Model 1 (How sick?): percentage of area affected by E. carotovora (severity).
  • Model 2 (How often?): frequency of disease incidence (number of cases).

Data were natural-log (ln) transformed in SPSS; outliers removed using box plots.

Supporting methods

  • Kernel density: "worst spots" hotspot map (mapping only).
  • Near tool: distance to nearest road, river, irrigation line and elevation contour.
  • Moran's I: clustering check. Road distance and elevation first showed clustering.
  • R² / Adjusted R²: model fit. VIF: all below 7.5.
  • Joint F: overall significance. Koenker (BP): not significant, so GWR was not needed.

About this sample

Boundaries: PSGC (faeldon/philippines-json-maps). Rivers and canals: HOTOSM waterways. Roads: OpenStreetMap. Elevation: ALOS AW3D30. Processed in QGIS. Farm points, weather values and OLS numbers are placeholders (OLS values from Idris et al., 2023, Johor).
